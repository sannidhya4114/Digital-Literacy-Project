# Do’s
1. Audit your privacy settings regularly to ensure you know exactly who can see your personal posts and tagged photos.
2. Network with intent. Follow industry leaders, join professional groups, and share content related to your field of study.
3. Fact-check before sharing. Verify the credibility of news or academic resources to maintain your own reputation for accuracy.
4. Highlight your milestones. Use your platform to showcase course projects, certifications, volunteer work, or club involvements.
5. Use a professional headshot. Even on semi-private platforms, a clean, recognizable profile picture helps establish a consistent "brand."

# Don'ts
1. Don't post during class or internships. It signals a lack of engagement and can be seen as a sign of poor time management.
2. Don't post "vent" sessions. Complaining about professors, specific courses, or previous employers can raise red flags for future hiring managers.
3. Don't engage with "trolls" or toxic threads. Heated public arguments rarely age well and can be taken out of context later.
4. Don't share sensitive personal data. Avoid posting your student ID number, specific dorm location, or full birthdate to prevent identity theft.
5. Don't ignore the "Grandmother Rule." If you wouldn't want a family member or a future boss to see it, it probably shouldn't be public.
